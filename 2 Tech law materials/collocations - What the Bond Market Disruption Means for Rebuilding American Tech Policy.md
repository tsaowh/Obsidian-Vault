| **a safe haven asset**                      | 避險資產；財經英文很常見                             | Government bonds are often regarded as **safe haven assets** during periods of economic uncertainty.                  | 在經濟不確定時期，政府債券通常被視為**避險資產**。       |
| **fiscal soundness**                        | 財政健全、財政穩健；比 _financial stability_ 更偏政府財政 | Investors are beginning to question the **fiscal soundness** of countries with rapidly rising debt.                   | 投資人開始質疑那些債務迅速增加國家的**財政健全程度**。     |
| **when the bill comes due**                 | 到了必須付出代價／償還債務的時候；很漂亮的隱喻                  | Governments can postpone difficult reforms, but eventually **the bill comes due**.                                    | 政府可以延後艱難的改革，但最終仍會**到了必須付出代價的時候**。 |
| **fiscal consolidation**                    | 財政整頓、財政鞏固；正式政策用語                         | The government may have little choice but to pursue **fiscal consolidation** through higher taxes and lower spending. | 政府可能別無選擇，只能透過提高稅收與削減支出進行**財政整頓**。 |
| **be placed under extreme budget pressure** | 面臨極大的預算壓力                                | Long-term research programs could **be placed under extreme budget pressure** during a fiscal crisis.                 | 在財政危機期間，長期研究計畫可能會**面臨極大的預算壓力**。   |
| **eye-popping increases in…**               | ……驚人的／令人瞠目結舌的增長；評論文章很好用                  | The country has seen **eye-popping increases in defense spending** over the past decade.                              | 過去十年間，該國的國防支出出現了**驚人的增長**。        |
| **the timeline has advanced substantially** | 時程大幅提前；原本以為很久以後才發生，現在更早到來                | Climate change has accelerated so quickly that **the timeline has advanced substantially**.                           | 氣候變遷加速得如此迅速，以致原先預期的時程已經**大幅提前**。  |
| **the relative breakdown of…**              | ……的相對組成／比例分布                             | Before discussing reforms, we need to understand **the relative breakdown of public spending**.                       | 在討論改革之前，我們必須先了解公共支出的**相對組成**。     |
| **interest servicing the debt**             | 償付債務利息                                   | More government revenue is now being consumed by **interest servicing the debt**.                                     | 如今愈來愈多政府收入被用於**償付債務利息**。          |
| **engage in fiscal consolidation**          | 採取／進行財政整頓                                | A future administration may be forced to **engage in fiscal consolidation**.                                          | 未來的政府可能被迫**進行財政整頓**。              |
| **wipe out investments in…**                | 徹底砍掉／消除對……的投資；語氣強                        | Cutting the budget too aggressively could **wipe out investments in basic science**.                                  | 過度激進地削減預算可能會**徹底砍掉對基礎科學的投資**。     |
| **get its fiscal house in order**           | 整頓好自己的財政；非常道地的政策慣用語                      | The country needs to **get its fiscal house in order** before launching another massive spending program.             | 該國在推出另一項大規模支出計畫之前，必須先**整頓好自身財政**。 |

| **a leading indicator of…**             | ……的領先指標／早期訊號             | Consumer confidence can serve as **a leading indicator of economic weakness**.                     | 消費者信心可以作為經濟疲弱的**領先指標**。         |
| **a dependable force for…**             | 在……方面值得依賴的力量             | For decades, the country portrayed itself as **a dependable force for regional stability**.        | 幾十年來，該國一直將自己塑造成維護區域穩定的**可靠力量**。 |
| **make bets on…**                       | 把希望／資金／政治籌碼押在……上         | Smaller countries are increasingly reluctant to **make long-term bets on American leadership**.    | 較小的國家愈來愈不願意**長期押注於美國的領導力**。     |
| **a loss of influence and trust**       | 影響力與信任的流失                | Institutional instability can lead to **a loss of influence and trust** abroad.                    | 制度不穩定可能造成該國在海外**影響力與信任的流失**。    |
| **the toolbox policymakers have to…**   | 政策制定者用來……的一整套政策工具        | Sanctions are only one part of **the toolbox policymakers have to influence foreign governments**. | 制裁只是政策制定者用來影響外國政府的**政策工具箱**之一。  |
| **reduce one's exposure to…**           | 降低對……的曝險／依賴程度            | European governments are seeking to **reduce their exposure to foreign technology providers**.     | 歐洲各國政府正設法**降低對外國科技供應商的依賴與曝險**。  |
| **a realignment toward…**               | 重新調整、轉而靠向……              | The crisis could accelerate **a realignment toward regional alliances**.                           | 這場危機可能加速各國**重新轉向區域聯盟**。         |
| **serve as a credible deterrent to…**   | 成為對……具有可信度的嚇阻力量          | Military capability matters only if it can **serve as a credible deterrent to aggression**.        | 軍事能力只有在能對侵略行為**形成可信嚇阻**時才真正重要。  |
| **be paired with…**                     | 與……同時出現／結合；分析文章很好用       | High debt, **paired with** weak economic growth, severely limits policy options.                   | 高債務**再加上**疲弱的經濟成長，會嚴重限制政策選項。    |
| **make something much more precarious** | 使某事變得更加岌岌可危／不穩定          | Political polarization has **made the situation much more precarious**.                            | 政治兩極化使情勢變得**更加岌岌可危**。           |
| **have deleterious effects on…**        | 對……產生有害／不利影響；很正式         | Excessive austerity can **have deleterious effects on economic growth**.                           | 過度的財政緊縮可能**對經濟成長造成不利影響**。       |
| **be on the ropes**                     | 陷入困境、搖搖欲墜；來自拳擊，「被逼到擂台繩邊」 | The domestic semiconductor industry was already **on the ropes** before the crisis began.          | 在危機爆發以前，國內半導體產業就已經**岌岌可危**。     |
| **reckon with the fact that…**          | 正視／不得不面對……這個事實；非常值得學     | Policymakers must **reckon with the fact that** resources are finite.                              | 政策制定者必須**正視資源有限這個事實**。          |

| **pull together a plan**             | 整合各方意見擬出一套計畫                                                         | Researchers are **pulling together a plan** for responsible AI governance.                                    | 研究人員正在**整合並擬定一套**負責任 AI 治理方案。                |
| **a nuts-and-bolts approach to…**    | 螺帽和螺栓 引伸為 務實、著重具體執行細節的方法；很漂亮                                         | AI regulation needs **a nuts-and-bolts approach to implementation**, not merely broad principles.             | AI 監管需要的是**著重具體執行細節的方法**，而不只是宏大的原則。          |

| **the consequences of inaction**     | 不採取行動的後果                                                             | Voters need to understand both the cost of reform and **the consequences of inaction**.                       | 選民需要了解改革的成本，以及**不採取行動所造成的後果**。               |
| **far outweigh the costs**           | 遠遠超過其成本；正式論證超好用                                                      | The long-term benefits of scientific investment **far outweigh the costs**.                                   | 科學投資的長期效益**遠遠超過其成本**。                        |
| **some of our most vexing problems** | 我們最棘手、最難解決的一些問題                                                      | AI may help address **some of our most vexing social problems**, but it can also create new ones.             | AI 或許能協助解決**一些最棘手的社會問題**，但也可能製造新的問題。         |
| **give someone the best shot at…**   | 給某人最大的機會去……                                                          | Good education gives young people **the best shot at building a better future**.                              | 良好的教育能讓年輕人擁有**打造更好未來的最佳機會**。                 |
| **muster**                           | 集結／使出所能調動的一切力量；常見於 _all the courage/resources/support we can muster_ | We should mobilize **all the political will we can muster** to address climate change.                        | 我們應該調動**所有能夠集結的政治意志**來因應氣候變遷。                |
