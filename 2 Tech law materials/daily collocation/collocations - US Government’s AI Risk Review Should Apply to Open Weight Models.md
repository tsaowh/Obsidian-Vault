
| 英文詞語搭配                                                             | 中文意思／語感                                   | 例句                                                                                                              | 翻譯                               |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **little is known about...**                                       | 對……所知甚少；很適合新聞、政策分析開頭                      | **Little is known about** how the agency evaluates high-risk AI systems.                                        | 外界對該機關如何評估高風險 AI 系統所知甚少。         |
| **one salient detail**                                             | 一個特別突出、值得注意的細節；比 _important detail_ 更有分析味 | **One salient detail** is that the proposal applies only to commercial providers.                               | 一個值得注意的細節是，該提案只適用於商業供應商。         |
| **in the public domain**                                           | 已公開、屬公眾可取得資訊                              | Much of the evidence is already **in the public domain**.                                                       | 許多證據其實早已公開。                      |
| **stem from...**                                                   | 源自、肇因於……；比 _come from_ 正式                 | Public distrust often **stems from** a lack of transparency.                                                    | 公眾的不信任往往源自缺乏透明度。                 |
| **defend the value of...**                                         | 為……的價值辯護                                  | Researchers have repeatedly **defended the value of open scientific collaboration**.                            | 研究人員一再為開放科學合作的價值辯護。              |
| **promote competition**                                            | 促進競爭；政策、經濟文常用                             | Open standards can **promote competition** by lowering barriers to entry.                                       | 開放標準可以透過降低進入障礙來促進競爭。             |
| **adapt A to a variety of specialized uses**                       | 將 A 調整、適配到各種專門用途                          | Smaller firms can **adapt general-purpose models to a variety of specialized uses**.                            | 小型企業可以把通用模型調整至各種專門用途。            |
| **a vital part of a diverse and vibrant ecosystem**                | 多元而有活力的生態系中不可或缺的一環                        | Independent developers are **a vital part of a diverse and vibrant technology ecosystem**.                      | 獨立開發者是多元而有活力的科技生態系中不可或缺的一環。      |
| **in retaliation for...**                                          | 為報復……而採取措施；國際政治、貿易常見                      | The government imposed additional tariffs **in retaliation for** the new restrictions.                          | 政府為報復新的限制措施而加徵關稅。                |
| **overshoot the mark**                                             | 做過頭、矯枉過正；非常漂亮的評論用語                        | Regulation is necessary, but an outright ban would **overshoot the mark**.                                      | 監管確有必要，但全面禁止就有些矯枉過正了。            |
| **it does not follow that...**                                     | 並不能因此推出……；邏輯論證超實用                         | The technology has legitimate uses, but **it does not follow that** it should remain entirely unregulated.      | 這項技術有正當用途，但不能因此推出它就應完全不受監管。      |
| **pose significant, foreseeable risks**                            | 帶來重大且可預見的風險                               | Autonomous systems may **pose significant, foreseeable risks** when deployed without adequate safeguards.       | 自主系統若在缺乏充分防護措施下部署，可能帶來重大且可預見的風險。 |
| **reduce risks to acceptable levels**                              | 將風險降低至可接受程度；風險治理標準語言                      | Operators should demonstrate that they have **reduced safety risks to acceptable levels**.                      | 業者應證明已將安全風險降低到可接受程度。             |
| **regardless of whether...**                                       | 不論是否……；法律論證很好用                            | The rule should apply **regardless of whether** the software is distributed for free.                           | 無論軟體是否免費散布，該規則都應適用。              |
| **get around to doing something**                                  | 終於著手做某事；帶一點「拖很久才做」的語氣                     | Congress may eventually **get around to establishing** a comprehensive AI framework.                            | 國會或許最終總算會著手建立一套完整的 AI 規範架構。      |
| **with adequate standards of public accountability**               | 配合充分的公共問責標準                               | Any surveillance regime should operate **with adequate standards of public accountability**.                    | 任何監控制度都應在充分的公共問責標準下運作。           |
| **give rise to... / arise from...**                                | 引發……／源於……；法律學術文非常常見                       | Serious liability issues may **arise from** autonomous decision-making.                                         | 自主決策可能衍生嚴重的法律責任問題。               |
| **follow instructions in unintended ways**                         | 以非預期方式遵循指令                                | AI agents may **follow instructions in unintended ways** when objectives are poorly specified.                  | 當目標設定不明確時，AI 代理可能以非預期方式遵循指令。     |
| **attempt to cover one’s tracks**                                  | 試圖掩蓋行蹤／消除證據                               | The attacker deleted the logs in an attempt to **cover his tracks**.                                            | 攻擊者刪除紀錄，試圖掩蓋自己的行蹤。               |
| **be walled off from...**                                          | 與……隔離；資安、系統架構很好用                          | The testing environment should be completely **walled off from production systems**.                            | 測試環境應與正式系統完全隔離。                  |
| **spin out of control**                                            | 失去控制、局勢失控                                 | Poorly constrained autonomous agents could **spin out of control** under unusual conditions.                    | 缺乏充分限制的自主代理在特殊情況下可能失去控制。         |
| **whatever rationale makes A important also applies to B**         | 使 A 有必要的理由，同樣適用於 B；非常好的比較論證句型             | **Whatever rationale makes transparency important for governments also applies to powerful private platforms.** | 任何使政府透明度具有重要性的理由，同樣也適用於強大的私人平台。  |
| **have teeth**                                                     | 真正有執行力、有實效，而非紙上談兵                         | Data protection rules need meaningful penalties if they are to **have teeth**.                                  | 資料保護規定若要真正有執行力，就必須附帶有意義的處罰。      |
| **be accompanied by...**                                           | 必須伴隨、搭配……                                 | New regulatory powers should **be accompanied by** stronger judicial oversight.                                 | 新的監管權力應同時搭配更強的司法監督。              |
| **drive something underground**                                    | 把某活動逼入地下，使其轉為秘密、非法運作                      | Excessively strict regulation may **drive the market underground** rather than eliminate it.                    | 過度嚴格的管制可能只是把市場逼入地下，而非真正消除它。      |
| **outside the reach of... / where the law cannot easily reach**    | 超出……管轄／法律難以觸及                             | Digital services can operate from jurisdictions **where domestic law cannot easily reach them**.                | 數位服務可以在本國法律難以觸及的司法管轄區內營運。        |
| **as long as the common interest is clear, narrow, and technical** | 只要共同利益明確、範圍狹窄且具技術性                        | International cooperation is possible **as long as the common interest is clear, narrow, and technical**.       | 只要共同利益明確、範圍有限且具技術性，國際合作仍然可能。     |
| **a permanent part of the landscape**                              | 成為整體格局中長期存在的一部分                           | AI governance will likely become **a permanent part of the regulatory landscape**.                              | AI 治理很可能成為監管格局中長期存在的一部分。         |
| **make compliance with A a condition of access to B**              | 把遵守 A 作為進入 B 的條件；法律政策寫作非常強                | Regulators could **make compliance with cybersecurity standards a condition of access to the market**.          | 監管機關可以把遵守資安標準作為進入市場的條件。          |
| **give away leverage**                                             | 白白放棄談判籌碼／槓桿                               | Exempting major firms too early may **give away valuable regulatory leverage**.                                 | 過早豁免大型企業可能等於白白放棄寶貴的監管籌碼。         |
| **short-sighted and counterproductive**                            | 短視且適得其反；評論文章很好用                           | A blanket prohibition would be **short-sighted and counterproductive**.                                         | 全面禁止可能既短視又適得其反。                  |
| **abandon a policy / approach / posture**                          | 放棄某項政策／作法／立場                              | The government should **abandon its current approach** and adopt a risk-based framework.                        | 政府應放棄目前的作法，改採風險導向的規範架構。          |


- **one salient detail** — 一個值得特別注意的細節
- **stem from** — 源自於
- **overshoot the mark** — 做過頭、矯枉過正
- **it does not follow that...** — 並不能因此推出……
- **pose significant and foreseeable risks** — 帶來重大且可預見風險
- **reduce risks to acceptable levels** — 將風險降至可接受程度
- **with adequate standards of public accountability** — 具有充分公共問責標準
- **spin out of control** — 失控
- **have teeth** — 具有實質執行力
- **drive something underground** — 把某事逼入地下
- **make compliance with A a condition of access to B** — 將遵守 A 作為進入 B 的條件
- **short-sighted and counterproductive** — 短視且適得其反
