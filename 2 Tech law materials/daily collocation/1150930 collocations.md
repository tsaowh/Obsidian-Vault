
# 今日主題：讓政策從構想走向落實

### 1

**表達：** **translate evidence into policy and practice**

**意思／語感：** 將研究證據轉化為實際政策及日常作業，而非停留在論文或原則層次。

**例子：** The new unit will **translate evidence on algorithmic bias into procurement policy and day-to-day practice**.

**翻譯：** 新設單位將把演算法偏誤的研究證據，轉化為採購政策與日常實務。

**用法：** 基本結構為 _translate A into B_；_policy and practice_ 常並列，分別指正式規範與實際執行。

**Source：** [BMJ Quality & Safety：Translating evidence into policy and practice](https://qualitysafety.bmj.com/content/32/5/251?utm_source=chatgpt.com) 討論研究證據如何進入政策與實務。[BMJ Quality & Safety](https://qualitysafety.bmj.com/content/32/5/251?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### 2

**表達：** **gain traction**

**意思／語感：** 某項構想、政策或技術開始獲得支持、採用或實際進展；通常暗示先前推動不易。

**例子：** A proposal for mandatory AI-incident reporting is **gaining traction among state regulators**.

**翻譯：** 強制通報 AI 事件的提案，正逐漸獲得各州監管機關支持。

**用法：** 主詞常為 _proposal, idea, movement, technology_；可說 _gain traction among/with_ 某群體，或 _gain traction in_ 某領域。

**Source：** [Harvard Safra Center：AI Governance at a Crossroads](https://www.ethics.harvard.edu/news/2025/11/ai-governance-crossroads-americas-ai-action-plan-and-its-impact-businesses?utm_source=chatgpt.com) 使用 _did not gain traction_ 描述監管提案未能取得支持。[ethics.harvard.edu](https://www.ethics.harvard.edu/news/2025/11/ai-governance-crossroads-americas-ai-action-plan-and-its-impact-businesses?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### 3

**表達：** **run up against**

**意思／語感：** 在推進過程中碰上限制、阻力或難以迴避的矛盾。

**例子：** The data-sharing initiative **ran up against** statutory limits on the disclosure of personal information.

**翻譯：** 該資料共享計畫碰上了個資揭露方面的法定限制。

**用法：** 後面常接 _limits, constraints, opposition, practical difficulties_；比 _encounter_ 更具「推進時撞上阻礙」的動態感。

**Source：** [Lawfare：The Political Limits of China’s AI Diffusion Ambitions](https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/the-political-limits-of-china-s-ai-diffusion-ambitions?utm_source=chatgpt.com) 以 _run up against its commitment to employment stability_ 描述政策目標間的結構性衝突。[Lawfare](https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/the-political-limits-of-china-s-ai-diffusion-ambitions?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### 4

**表達：** **be ill-equipped to**

**意思／語感：** 缺乏完成某項任務所需的能力、工具、制度或準備；不一定是完全無能，而是現有條件不足。

**例子：** Small municipalities may be **ill-equipped to audit** complex AI systems without shared technical support.

**翻譯：** 若無共享的技術支援，小型地方政府可能缺乏稽核複雜 AI 系統的能力。

**用法：** 後接原形動詞；也可用 _be ill-equipped for + 名詞_。適合分析制度能力，不宜用來直接貶抑個人。

**Source：** [The Canadian Press／CityNews：Key federal agencies ill-equipped to fight growing cybercrime threat](https://toronto.citynews.ca/2024/06/04/key-federal-agencies-ill-equipped-to-fight-growing-cybercrime-threat-auditor-finds/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) 報導以此描述機關缺乏必要能力與工具。[toronto.citynews.ca](https://toronto.citynews.ca/2024/06/04/key-federal-agencies-ill-equipped-to-fight-growing-cybercrime-threat-auditor-finds/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### 5

**表達：** **reconcile competing demands**

**意思／語感：** 協調數項彼此衝突、但各有正當性的要求，使其能共存或形成可行方案。

**例子：** The agency must **reconcile competing demands** for rapid service delivery, privacy, and individualized review.

**翻譯：** 該機關必須協調快速提供服務、保障隱私及個別審查等相互競合的要求。

**用法：** 比 _balance different needs_ 更正式，也更強調尋找相容安排；常見於預算、監管及制度設計。

**Source：** [Brookings：Why is federal spending so hard to cut?](https://www.brookings.edu/articles/why-is-federal-spending-so-hard-to-cut-recurring-debt-ceiling-fights-will-only-be-solved-by-budget-reform/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) 使用 _reconciling competing demands_ 描述預算制度的核心能力。[Brookings](https://www.brookings.edu/articles/why-is-federal-spending-so-hard-to-cut-recurring-debt-ceiling-fights-will-only-be-solved-by-budget-reform/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### 6

**表達：** **close the gap between policy and practice**

**意思／語感：** 縮小正式政策與第一線實際執行情況之間的落差。

**例子：** Training alone will not **close the gap between policy and practice** if local offices remain understaffed.

**翻譯：** 如果地方辦公室仍然人力不足，單靠培訓無法縮小政策與實務之間的落差。

**用法：** 也可替換為 _bridge/narrow the gap between A and B_；_close_ 不必然表示徹底消除，也常指顯著縮小。

**Source：** [What Works Hub for Global Education：Understanding gaps between policy and practice](https://www.wwhge.org/resources/understanding-gaps-between-policy-and-practice/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) 明確使用此搭配討論政策執行落差。[What Works Hub for Global Education](https://www.wwhge.org/resources/understanding-gaps-between-policy-and-practice/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### 7

**表達：** **build institutional capacity to**

**意思／語感：** 建立組織長期執行某項職能所需的人力、知識、程序與制度，而非只提供一次性資源。

**例子：** The grants should help regulators **build institutional capacity to inspect** high-risk automated systems.

**翻譯：** 這些補助應協助監管機關建立查核高風險自動化系統的制度能力。

**用法：** _institutional capacity_ 的範圍通常比單純的 _staffing_ 廣，可能包括權限、協調機制、技術工具及專業知識。

**Source：** [OECD：Institutional Capacity and Skills](https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/sub-issues/policy-coherence-for-sustainable-development/institutional-capacity-and-skills.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com) 將政治與行政機制、流程、制度及工具視為政府能力的核心。[OECD](https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/sub-issues/policy-coherence-for-sustainable-development/institutional-capacity-and-skills.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### 8

**表達：** **come at the expense of**

**意思／語感：** 某項成果是以犧牲另一項利益、價值或群體為代價取得的。

**例子：** Faster regulatory approval should not **come at the expense of** procedural fairness.

**翻譯：** 加快監管核准，不應以犧牲程序公平為代價。

**用法：** 常用於指出隱藏的取捨；典型結構為 _A comes at the expense of B_。語氣通常帶批判或警示意味。

**Source：** [Lawfare：Narrowing the National Security Exception to Federal AI Guardrails](https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/narrowing-the-national-security-exception-to-federal-ai-guardrails?utm_source=chatgpt.com) 引述政策文件強調 AI 創新不應以人民利益或信任為代價。[Lawfare](https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/narrowing-the-national-security-exception-to-federal-ai-guardrails?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### 9

**表達：** **move the needle on**

**意思／語感：** 對某項重要指標或棘手問題產生足以被察覺的實質影響，而非只有象徵性進展。

**例子：** Publishing model cards will not **move the needle on accountability** unless agencies can verify the claims they contain.

**翻譯：** 除非機關能查證模型卡中的聲明，否則公布模型卡不會真正改善課責。

**用法：** 常見於商業、政策與評論英文，屬半正式慣用語；尤其常出現在否定句或疑問句中。

**Source：** [The Wall Street Journal：Will Looser Marijuana Rules Help Biden Move the Needle With Young Voters?](https://www.wsj.com/politics/elections/will-looser-marijuana-rules-help-biden-move-the-needle-with-young-voters-c59463bb?utm_source=chatgpt.com) 以此表達政策能否造成顯著影響。[wsj.com](https://www.wsj.com/politics/elections/will-looser-marijuana-rules-help-biden-move-the-needle-with-young-voters-c59463bb?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### 10

**表達：** **bring a goal within reach**

**意思／語感：** 透過某項措施，使原本遙遠或難以達成的目標變得可望實現。

**例子：** Shared technical infrastructure could **bring secure AI adoption within reach of** smaller public agencies.

**翻譯：** 共用技術基礎設施，可以使小型公務機關也有望安全採用 AI。

**用法：** 可用 _bring X within reach_；若指出受益者，接 _within reach of + 人或組織_。反義表達為 _put/push X out of reach_。

**Source：** [World Bank：Global poverty and climate goals will remain out of reach](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/opendata/global-poverty-and-climate-goals-will-remain-out-of-reach-with-b?utm_source=chatgpt.com) 使用 _bring that goal within reach_ 說明政策如何使目標變得可實現。[blogs.worldbank.org](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/opendata/global-poverty-and-climate-goals-will-remain-out-of-reach-with-b?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
