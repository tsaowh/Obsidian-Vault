
| 詞語搭配                                      | 中文語感            | 例句                                                                                                                                     |

| **an unresolved tension between A and B** | A 與 B 之間尚未化解的張力 | There is **an unresolved tension between individual freedom and public security**. 個人自由與公共安全之間仍存在尚未化解的張力。                              |
| **strike a balance between A and B**      | 在 A、B 之間取得平衡    | A democratic society must **strike a balance between freedom of expression and the protection of reputation**. 民主社會必須在言論自由與名譽保障之間取得平衡。 |

| **tip the balance toward**                | 使權衡的天平倒向……      | The emergence of new evidence may **tip the balance toward a different conclusion**. 新證據的出現可能使整體判斷轉向不同的結論。                             |
| **at the cost of**                        | 以犧牲……為代價        | Economic growth should not be achieved **at the cost of environmental sustainability**. 經濟成長不應以犧牲環境永續為代價。                              |

**be well positioned to do something**  
＝ 處於很有利的位置，可以做某事。

> People with diverse life experiences are often **well positioned to recognize perspectives that others overlook**.  
> 擁有多元人生經驗的人，往往更有條件看見他人容易忽略的觀點。

**at the technological cutting edge**  
＝ 處於科技最前沿。

> Law often struggles to keep pace with developments **at the technological cutting edge**.  
> 法律往往難以跟上科技最前沿的發展。

**be far from immune to**  
＝ 遠非不受……影響／自己也難以避免。

> Even experienced decision-makers are **far from immune to cognitive bias**.  
> 即使經驗豐富的決策者，也遠非不受認知偏誤影響。

**spend our energy understanding A rather than B**  
＝ 與其把力氣花在 B，不如真正理解 A。

> Perhaps we should **spend our energy understanding why institutions behave as they do, rather than imagining how we wish they behaved**.  
> 或許我們應該把精力放在理解制度為何如此運作，而不是想像我們希望它如何運作。
